# Doodle Shield – store metadata (copy into the Playables Developer Portal)

**Title:** Doodle Shield

**Short description (one line):**
Draw shapes that drop to the ground and protect the cutest animals!

**Long description:**
Draw a wall, a roof or a ramp with your finger – and watch it fall into place exactly as you drew it!
Cute baby animals need your help: stop the rolling attackers, shelter them from the rain and block
the flying shots. Every level asks for a different shape, and the levels never run out.
Relax with soft ASMR-style sounds, pastel colours and a friendly puppy, kitten, bunny, penguin and frog.

**Genre / category:** Brain & Puzzle (casual, physics, drawing)

**Keywords:** draw puzzle, physics puzzle, cute animals, relaxing, ASMR, draw to save, one finger, casual

**How to play (in one sentence):** Draw a shape, let it fall, and keep the animal safe until the timer ends.

**Controls:** touch / mouse – press and drag to draw, release to drop the shape.

**Orientation:** portrait or landscape (the game scales; no state is lost on resize).

**Audience:** everyone – no violence beyond cartoon bees, snowballs, ghosts and bats; no text chat, no links, no purchases.

**Save data:** one tiny JSON object (current level), well under the 3 MB limit.
